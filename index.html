Paulo Strobel

CFD Engineer | PhD in Aerospace Engineering

PhD in Aerospace Engineering

CFD & Thermal Optimization

I am a Mechanical Engineer with a PhD in Aerospace Engineering from Cranfield University. With over 10 years of experience in CFD, I specialize in delivering efficient solutions through advanced computational modeling.

I work with industry-leading CFD tools to solve challenging fluid dynamics problems and deliver innovative solutions.

I am open to CFD consulting projects and research collaborations in thermal optimization, HVAC systems, and multiphysics simulations.

Areas of Expertise: Computational Fluid Dynamics | Software Development | Rotor Aerodynamics | Data Center Thermal Optimization | HVAC Systems Analysis | Multiphysics Simulations | Thermal Management | Aerodynamic Optimization
## Projects

Plenum-Driven Thermal Optimization for 2.4 MW Data Center

Redesigned air distribution architecture using plenum-based flow control, achieving significant thermal compliance gains across a high-density facility.

Scala Data Center / Torres Eng.

CFD-Driven Pre-Construction Thermal Design for 5 MW Data Center

Full-scale pre-construction CFD analysis that delivered a 247% improvement in thermal compliance, preventing costly post-build corrections for a hyperscale facility.

Scala Data Center / Torres Eng.

Multi-Fidelity CFD & ML Optimization of Oleo-Pneumatic Landing Gear — LANDOne

Developed a modular multi-fidelity simulation framework for next-generation landing gear, integrating machine learning to accelerate shock absorber design and reduce computational cost.

Airbus/Cranfield University

Thermal CFD Optimization for Embraer Aerospace Manufacturing Data Center

Parametric CFD study of supply air angles and hot/cold aisle containment strategies, identifying the optimal cooling configuration for a mission-critical aerospace facility.

Embraer / Fox Eng.

Hyperscale Thermal Validation for Equinix — 3.35 MW Data Center

CFD thermal analysis and perforated tile flow validation for a 3.35 MW hyperscale facility, ensuring cooling reliability and airflow uniformity across high-density server rows.

Equinix / Torres Eng.

Mission-Critical Thermal CFD for 3 MW Banking Infrastructure — Banco do Brasil

Identified and resolved critical thermal imbalances between cooling capacity and rack density in a 3 MW banking data center, ensuring uptime reliability for mission-critical workloads.

Banco do Brasil/Fox Eng.

Hydrokinetic Turbine Design & Optimization – Tucunaré Project

Multi-institutional hydrokinetic energy initiative — cavitation-free BEM optimization, CFD wake analysis, and diffuser-augmented design for 250 kW–1 MW current turbines downstream of Brazil's largest Amazon dam

Eletronorte / University of Brasília
